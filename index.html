Tern privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Tern is an AI assistant that runs on your phone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your chats stay on your phone, there are no accounts, ads or analytics, and nothing is sent to us unless you choose to email us.

What stays on your phone

  • Chats, photos and files you attach, and the replies.
  • Memory: notes from earlier chats that Tern uses to recall what you talked about, and facts it learns about you (for example your name or a pet's name). You can see and delete these in Settings (under What … remembers, named after your assistant), or turn memory off.
  • Settings, such as the name you gave the assistant and the voice you chose.

The AI that writes replies runs entirely on the phone. This data is stored in Tern's private storage, is not backed up to the cloud and is not copied to a new phone. It is deleted when you delete it in the app or uninstall Tern.

When Tern uses the internet

Tern works offline. It only connects to the internet in these cases:

  • Web search (on by default; you can turn it off in Settings). When a question needs current information, Tern sends the words of the search, never your conversation, photos or files, directly from your phone to one of these services: DuckDuckGo, Mojeek, Wikipedia, Google News or Open-Meteo (for weather, the place name you asked about). If a message contains a link, or you share a web page to Tern, it downloads that page to read it. These services receive your phone's internet address, as with any website, and their own privacy policies apply.
  • Getting the AI model. When installed from Google Play, Google Play downloads Tern's AI model after installation. Copies of Tern from elsewhere can download the model from Hugging Face. This sends no personal information beyond what any download involves.
  • Voice input. Tern uses your phone's speech recognition. It asks for on-device recognition, so your voice stays on the phone where the phone supports that. On phones without it, your phone's speech service (for example Google's) may process the audio under its own privacy policy. For spoken replies Tern picks voices stored on the phone.

With web search turned off, the only other connections are the ones above that you choose to use.

What we receive

Nothing, unless you contact us. Reporting a reply opens your email app with the reason, your message and the reply filled in, addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. If you do, we use it only to look into the problem and improve Tern, keep it only as long as that takes, and don't share it.

Tern has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't sell or share your information.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only when you use voice typing or a voice conversation.
  • Notifications: to show the progress of a model download.
  • Internet: for the uses above.

Tern does not ask for your location, contacts or photos; you choose what to attach.

Children

Tern is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and at the same web address, with a new effective date.
